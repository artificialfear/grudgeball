TEAMS

HEARTS & TIMERS

If time runs out, that team automatically passes.
Sudden-death teams are out on a wrong answer either way.

QUESTIONS

10 per row. Big question sets get a random batch each game; small ones repeat a few questions to fill the board.

MUSIC QUESTIONS

Theory = AP-style ear training (intervals, chords, cadences, modes, meter, error detection) and theory reading. Auto picks Theory for "Theory" sets.
New questions are made every game.

RULES

Simple (beginners): 5 items (Shield, Reed, Metronome, Free Shots, Traumatic Scale), no gang-up rule, hold 1 item, 20 s to answer, 7 s to steal, no leader tax. Full: everything on.

FAIRNESS

Students number off 1, 2, 3… and the game calls who shoots next. Set to 0 to turn off.
Last place gets +1 heart when they open an item box.
A team at max hearts can't collect items — they pick again.
Hits, shields, revives and other item effects play over the team cards. Turn off if the board lags.
