# Set: Level 1 Beginner
# Beginners: Rank 3 vocab, note values, staff and clefs, piano landmarks, instruments
# One question per line:   Question | Answer
What is the speed of the beat called? | Tempo
What is the steady, underlying pulse of the music called? | The beat
What does forte (f) mean? | Loud
What does piano (p) mean? | Soft
What do we call silence in music? | A rest
What symbol raises a note a half step? | A sharp (♯)
What symbol lowers a note a half step? | A flat (♭)
What symbol cancels a sharp or a flat? | A natural (♮)
What is the space between two bar lines called? | A measure
What divides music into measures? | A bar line
What does D.C. (Da Capo) mean? | Go back to the beginning
What is another name for the treble clef? | The G clef
What is another name for the bass clef? | The F clef
What tells how many counts are in each measure and which note gets one count? | The time signature
What are the sharps or flats at the beginning of a piece of music called? | The key signature
How many lines and how many spaces are on a music staff? | 5 lines and 4 spaces
In 4/4 time, how many beats does a whole note get? | 4 beats
In 4/4 time, how many beats does a half note get? | 2 beats
In 4/4 time, how many beats does a quarter note get? | 1 beat
In 4/4 time, how many beats does a quarter rest get? | 1 beat
In 4/4 time, how many beats does a whole rest get? | 4 beats (a whole measure)
How many quarter notes equal one half note? | 2
How many half notes equal one whole note? | 2
How many quarter notes equal one whole note? | 4
In 4/4 time, how many beats are in each measure? | 4
In 3/4 time, how many beats are in each measure? | 3
What does the top number of a time signature tell you? | How many beats are in each measure
When the bottom number of a time signature is 4, which note gets one beat? | The quarter note
How do we count four quarter notes in a measure of 4/4? | 1 2 3 4
What are the seven letters of the musical alphabet? | A B C D E F G
What letter comes after G in the musical alphabet? | A (the alphabet starts over)
On the piano, which white key is just to the left of the Chopsticks (2 black keys) group? | C
On the piano, which white key is just to the left of the Fork (3 black keys) group? | F
Name the treble clef lines from bottom to top. | E G B D F
Name the treble clef spaces from bottom to top. | F A C E
Name the bass clef lines from bottom to top. | G B D F A
Name the bass clef spaces from bottom to top. | A C E G
What is the shape of your lips and mouth when you play called? | Embouchure
Which clef do flutes, clarinets, saxophones and trumpets read? | Treble clef
Which clef do trombones, euphoniums, tubas and bassoons read? | Bass clef
What is the stick a conductor uses called? | A baton
Name the two woodwind instruments in our band that use a single reed. | Clarinet and saxophone
Which woodwind instrument uses no reed at all? | Flute (or piccolo)
